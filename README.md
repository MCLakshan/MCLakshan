## Research Focus

Everything below is really one interest seen from different angles — the pipeline that takes a rule, grows a structure from it, and renders the result fast enough to play.

- **Computer Graphics** — turning math into images
- **Procedural Content Generation** — worlds that build themselves from rules
- **Structural & Graph-Based Modeling** — shape as nodes, branches, and the rules between them
- **Machine Learning for 3D** — learning the growth instead of hand-scripting it
- **Real-Time Rendering** — all of the above, fast enough to render every frame
- **Game Development & Interactive Systems** — where the generated world stops being a dataset and becomes somewhere you can play
